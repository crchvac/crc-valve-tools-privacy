Privacy Policy

CRC Valve Tools · Last updated August 2026

CRC Valve Tools does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

What this app does

  • The app does not require an account, login, or any personal information to use.
  • Project and configuration data you enter (valve selections, project schedules, tags) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to CRC or any third party.
  • The app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
  • The app does not access your camera, contacts, or location.
  • If you use the app's share or export feature, any file you generate (e.g. a PDF or image) is created and shared using your device's own operating system — CRC does not receive a copy.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact CRC via crchvac.com/contact-us.
